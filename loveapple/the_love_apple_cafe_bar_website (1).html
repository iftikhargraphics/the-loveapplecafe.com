Our Heritage & Spirit

Welcome to
The Love Apple

Steeped in local culture, The Love Apple Cafe-Bar is owned and nurtured by Pav & Trina Skjoldan-Iqbal. Channeling the iconic energy of the original 1997 Love Apple venue, Pav and Trina have re-imagined the brand into a warm, inclusive, and relaxed sanctuary right in the heart of Saltaire.

Our ethos combines healthy baked & grilled wholesome dishes, artisan specialty coffees, locally brewed craft beers, and handcrafted evening cocktails. Blending Bauhaus aesthetic influences with Saltaire’s vibrant arts and music culture, we offer a relaxed daytime lounge that seamlessly evolves into a cozy evening bar with weekend vinyl DJ sets.
